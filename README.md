<div align="center">

## 📖 **How To Use**

</div>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🔗 **Method 1: Pair Code**

1. 🌐 Open the website
2. 📑 Click **Pair Code** tab
3. 📱 Enter WhatsApp number with country code
   - ✅ **Correct:** `923447861706`
   - ❌ **Wrong:** `+92 344 7861706`
4. 🔘 Click **Generate Pair Code**
5. 📋 Copy the 8-digit code
6. 📲 Open WhatsApp → **Settings** → **Linked Devices**
7. 🔗 Click **Link with phone number**
8. ⌨️ Enter the code
9. ✅ **Copy your Session ID** (`DEVIL~...`)

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🔳 **Method 2: QR Code**

1. 🌐 Open the website
2. 📑 Click **QR Code** tab
3. 🔘 Click **Generate QR Code**
4. 📲 Open WhatsApp → **Linked Devices**
5. 🔗 Click **Link a Device**
6. 📸 Scan the QR with your phone
7. ✅ **Copy your Session ID**

<br/>

> ⏱️ **Note:** QR expires in 20 seconds.  
> 🔄 Click **Refresh QR** if needed.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

<br/>

### 🤖 **Using Your Session ID**

Paste the generated session ID into your bot's config:

```env
SESSION_ID=DEVIL~eyJub2lzZUtleSI6eyJwcml2YXRlIjp7...
```

Then start your bot — **DONE!** 🎉
